Fragrance wardrobe

A proper home for the fragrances you own

Log every bottle with its notes, size and price. Record what you wear each day in a single tap. Over time, ScentKeep shows you what you actually reach for — and what has been sitting untouched at the back of the shelf.

Download on the App Store Free · iPhone · No account needed

Your wardrobe

Photo, house, note pyramid, size, price. Longevity and sillage ratings. Season and occasion tags.

Scent of the Day

One tap to log today's wear. Optional occasion, weather and mood. A dated diary with streaks.

How much is left

Live bottle levels worked out from your own wear history, a warning before a favourite runs dry, and an estimate of when each one runs out.

What should I wear?

A daily pick from your own shelf that tells you why — the season you tagged it for, how long it has rested, how you rated it.

Insights

Collection value, most-worn bottles, cost per wear, rotation, and what you've neglected for 90 days.

Find what you own

What else on your shelf smells like this, which two layer well, and which wishlist entries are really repeats. Worked out on your device.

Wishlist, split in two

What you mean to buy, and what you only mean to sniff first. Move one across the day you buy it.

Worth insuring

An itemised export with prices, dates and photos. Purchase prices only — it never pretends to be a valuation.

Built to respect you

  • No account required. Open it and start. No email, no sign-up.
  • Works offline. Your collection lives on your device.
  • No ads. Not now, not later.
  • The daily pick is free. So is sharing your shelf. Habit and word of mouth are not things to charge for.
  • Your data is yours. Export everything as a file, or delete it permanently, from inside the app.
